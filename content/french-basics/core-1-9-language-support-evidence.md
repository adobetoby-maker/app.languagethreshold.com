# French 101 Core 1–9 support-text alignment

The source-pinned Core 1–9 drafts remain unchanged. `core-1-9-language-support-overrides.json` records each original target/native pair, its derived French-course pair and a reason. The generator checks every original against the draft and its SHA-256 provenance before applying 16 card overrides, one dialogue override and one grammar-note override to the private fixture. `sourceCards`, `sourceReader`, `sourceDialogue` and `sourceGrammarNotes` preserve the original text inside the fixture. Lesson IDs, selected-card indexes and progress identities do not change.

The direct language conflict occurs in lesson 1's studying-Spanish sentence, lesson 3's messages-in-Spanish sentence, and four lesson 8–9 sentences where the French target says *français* but the inherited English says Spanish. The derived card and Reader text now says French on both sides. Lesson 3's *petite ville* pair now says “small town” in its card, Reader and dialogue. A few term glosses were narrowed: *en* no longer claims every “in/on/at” use, *dans* is “in/inside,” *par* is “by/through,” and *les* is labeled plural of either gender. The two original lesson 7 *les* cards remain in the source deck, with one in the practice backlog as previously documented.
