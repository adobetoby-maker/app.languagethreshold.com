# French 101 Core 10–12 draft checkpoint

This is French target / English support authored draft content for `french-basics`, stacked on Core 7–9 draft PR #253. It is not registered in the runtime. Stable stage-local lesson numbers and learner progress remain unchanged; `reviewed:false` is intentional.

The English objectives, 42 card/phrase meanings, and 18 Reader meanings come from pinned `src/data/spanish-basics-core.json` and `src/data/spanish-basics-practice.json` source. `source-english-core-10-12.json` records the full-file hashes and bounded lessons. The French draft follows the source's time, likes and wants progression, but teaches French **il est** clock time, subject-agreeing **aimer**, and irregular **vouloir** rather than transplanting Spanish `ser`, `gustar` or `querer` forms. Each lesson has six aligned contextual lines, a speaking task and five checks. Broad source glosses are retained verbatim for audit and explained with contextual notes.

`python3 scripts/validate-french-basics-core-10-12-draft.py` validates source hashes, exact English pairs, reading alignment, draft structure and honest review state. The script-only listening checks are not recordings or audio acceptance.
