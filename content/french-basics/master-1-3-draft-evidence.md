# French 101 Master 1–3 draft checkpoint

This French-target / English-support batch is stacked on draft PR #262. It is source-pinned authoring work only: no runtime registration, app assets, audio, or progress-ID changes. `reviewed:false` remains.

`source-english-master-1-3.json` pins the exact English objectives, 60 card meanings and 36 Reader meanings from `master-spanish-basics.json` and the Master practice source, with both whole-file SHA-256 hashes. `python3 scripts/validate-french-basics-master-1-3-draft.py` verifies each source item, every English pair, 12 aligned reading lines per lesson and the five-check structure. The builder regenerates both JSON files from pinned source and authored French.

**Curriculum blocker:** The existing Spanish Master lessons 1–3 repeat beginner introductions, classroom objects and plurals. Their English objectives include Spanish vowel pronunciation and Spanish plural rules, neither of which transfers directly to French. This draft preserves the English meanings while supplying French-specific grammar, register and sentence forms; it does not establish a credible Master-level progression. A curriculum redesign or explicit reclassification is needed before release. The English “hello” and “good morning” meanings both use French *bonjour*; there is no honest distinct universal French word for each card. `sac à dos` is masculine in French. “Spanish students” is interpreted as students of Spanish in the classroom context. These decisions require independent language review.

Each lesson has 20 cards, 12 aligned Reader lines, French grammar, a contextual speaking task and five checks including script-only listening. Prior Claude CLI review budget was exhausted, so no Fable/Opus review call was made. Independent French fidelity/curriculum review, qualified human review, six-mode runtime, Reader/cards/dictionary/speaking/offline integration, recorded audio and phone acceptance all remain open. This is **not a released Master course**.
