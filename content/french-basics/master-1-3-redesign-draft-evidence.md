# French 101 Master 1–3 redesigned draft checkpoint

This French-target / English-support authoring batch follows the [20-slot Master map](../../docs/french-program/french-101-master-redesign.md) and is stacked on its draft PR. The original `master-1-3-draft.json` and `source-english-master-1-3.json` remain unchanged as historical source-aligned drafts/provenance. The new `master-1-3-redesign-draft.json` is the **candidate** for future review, not a registered lesson pack. It preserves `master-french-basics:1`, `:2`, `:3`, `reviewed:false`, and account progress state. There are no recorded clips.

The redesigned lessons have 20 cards and 12 new aligned French/English Reader lines each. Master 1 introduces Camille to a group, answers why she joined and changes from informal `tu` to formal `vous` for the coordinator. Master 2 reconciles a written supplies brief with available items, proposes two ordinary notebooks for an unavailable large-format notebook and confirms the teacher’s condition. Master 3 allocates four classmates’ materials and negotiates the only dictionary between Ana and Hugo. Each has a role, opening, four objectives, complication, closure, grammar explanation and five checks.

`python3 scripts/validate-french-basics-master-redesign-1-3-draft.py` checks map/source/old-draft SHA-256 references, stable IDs, 60 exact new card pairs, 36 aligned Reader pairs, speaking and check structure, script-only listening status, and **zero exact English Reader-line reuse** from Spanish Foundation/Core/Master or the legacy French Master draft. It cannot establish naturalness or CEFR level. New English lines intentionally differ from the Spanish source; keeping the old pairs learner-facing would reintroduce its beginner repetition. Spanish source hashes remain immutable provenance only.

Independent French fidelity/curriculum review, register and tense review, qualified human review, six-mode/Reader/cards/dictionary/speaking/offline runtime integration, recorded audio and phone acceptance remain open. Claude review was not attempted because the earlier bounded CLI budget was exhausted. This is **not released Master content**.
