# French 101 Master 4–6 redesigned draft checkpoint

This French-target / English-support batch follows the proposed 20-slot Master curriculum map and stacks on the redesigned Master 1–3 draft. It preserves `master-french-basics:4`, `:5`, and `:6` and does not alter the Spanish source or the existing account progress model. The new `master-4-6-redesign-draft.json` is unregistered, `reviewed:false`, and has no recorded audio.

Each lesson has 8 vocabulary cards, 12 new French/English phrase cards aligned one-to-one with its 12 Reader lines, a speaking scenario, French grammar notes, and five draft checks. Master 4 distinguishes a continuing role from temporary availability in a meeting handoff. Master 5 compares a room's earlier and current state, then corrects an apparent missing-item report. Master 6 repairs an incorrectly repeated room reservation and leaves it pending until availability is confirmed. The dialogue uses a formal `vous` register with the clerk and informal `tu` between colleagues.

`python3 scripts/validate-french-basics-master-redesign-4-6-draft.py` checks stable IDs, curriculum objective and prerequisite linkage, SHA-256 references to the map and original Spanish course/practice and prior redesigned batch, 60 aligned cards, 36 aligned Reader pairs, speaking/check structure, script-only listening status, and zero exact English Reader-line reuse from Spanish Foundation/Core/Master or redesigned French Master 1–3. The new English pairs intentionally differ from the Spanish Master source because its earlier-stage repetition conflicts with the proposed Master-level progression. Legacy source is retained as provenance, not learner-facing text.

Independent French fidelity and curriculum review, register/tense/prerequisite review, qualified human review, six-mode runtime and offline integration, recorded audio, and device acceptance remain open. Claude review was not attempted because the earlier bounded CLI budget was exhausted. These are authored candidates, not released lessons.
