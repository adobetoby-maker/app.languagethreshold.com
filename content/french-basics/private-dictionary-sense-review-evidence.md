# French 101 private dictionary sense review — source pass

This bounded pass checks the 25 multiple-gloss headwords in private dictionary inventory PR #322 against the SHA-pinned authored lesson rows and, where present, their exact lesson-scoped support overlays. It does not change a French or English lesson word, merge a sense ID, set `reviewed:true`, or register the dictionary.

Thirteen cases have a **provisional annotation overlap** supported by the exact source rows: `gentil`, `gentille`, `la porte`, `la professeure`, `la table`, `s’il vous plaît`, `sur`, `trois`, `le`, `un cahier`, `un livre`, `un étudiant`, and `une étudiante`. This pass adds the final five after checking their lesson rows: each keeps the same core English meaning while its gloss varies by article or masculine/feminine annotation. For example, `un cahier` appears as “a notebook” and “notebook (masculine, singular noun)”; `un étudiant` appears as “a student (man)” and “student (masculine singular).” The review file records a source path, SHA-256, stage, lesson, word index, and exact gloss for every provisional sense, including overlay provenance where applicable. These pairs stay separate so existing IDs and lesson meanings remain stable.

The other 12 remain `unresolved`: `dans`, `dans / sur / à`, `elle`, `est`, `il`, `la`, `les`, `là`, `pour`, `un`, `une`, and `une solution de rechange`. They may involve different grammar roles, articles, context-specific meanings, or wording that requires a French educator's decision. All 25 retain `qualifiedFrenchReviewRequired:true`, `reviewed:false`, and `registered:false`; none is a published dictionary sense.

Rebuild and negative-check with `python3 scripts/build-french-101-private-sense-review.py --check` and `python3 scripts/test-french-101-private-sense-review.py`. The validator rejects changed source evidence, glosses, IDs, statuses, or review flags. Qualified French review must decide final POS, inflection, register and France/Québec variants before any learner-facing dictionary release. Audio, offline installation, browser/phone and V4 acceptance are separate gates.
