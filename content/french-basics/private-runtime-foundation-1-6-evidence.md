# French 101 Foundation 1–6 private runtime pilot

This fixture extends the unregistered Foundation 1–3 pilot through lessons 4–6 under the distinct stable ID `foundation-french-basics`. It lives in `content/french-basics/private-runtime-foundation-1-6.json`, outside the live `src/data/core60` stage directory and `CORE60_MANIFEST`, and remains `privatePilot:true`, `registered:false`, `reviewed:false`, with script-only audio. The earlier 1–3 fixture remains intact. No Spanish or Japanese module IDs, curricula, or learner progress data are changed.

The stage-file portion converts with `parseStageFile` and `stageCurriculumFromFile`. The sidecar preserves all exact French/English authored pairs from Foundation 1–3 and 4–6: 6 lessons, 134 cards, 49 Reader dialogue pairs, term dictionary entries, speaking briefs and checks. It supplies inputs for See, Recall, Hear, Type, Say and Write; Hear and Say have no recordings. Both authored drafts and their Spanish sources are SHA-256 pinned.

Focused tests verify source hashes, distinct French/Spanish/Japanese module keys, absence from the live manifest, stage conversion, all six mode inputs and exact Reader/card/dictionary/speaking/check pairs. The **production stage validator does not pass** this private fixture: it requires ten Foundation lessons and reports oversized term lists in lessons 2–6 and oversized phrase lists in lessons 5–6. The focused test records those exact eight errors and fails if unanticipated errors appear. A later release step should curate 8–12 stage terms and 4–6 stage phrases per lesson while retaining any useful extra terms in a separate dictionary/deck, then complete lessons 7–10 and rerun full validation. This pilot is not a release candidate.

Independent French fidelity/curriculum and qualified human review, full Foundation 1–10 content, language-pair-safe UI registration, offline packs, speech recognition and feedback, recorded French audio, signed-in browser and phone acceptance remain open.
