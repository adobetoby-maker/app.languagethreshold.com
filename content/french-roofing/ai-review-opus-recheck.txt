## OPEN material issues (AI scope)

**1. Internal process text is showing in learner-facing fields.** This is the remaining part of M12. The learner would see:
- L1 `regionalNotesEnglish[0]`: "This French-target adaptation changes that language name… preserving the original in reading.sourceNative."
- L3 `regionalNotesEnglish[2]`: "A qualified roofing safety reviewer must approve or replace… before this lesson can be released."
- L3 `grammarTeachingEnglish`, last sentence: "…is a source-aligned language example…"

Fix: move all three into an internal field such as `reviewNotesEnglish`. If a learner-facing line is still wanted, use something plain like: "This sentence practices the words. It does not certify any equipment. Follow your employer's checks."

**2. L2 says it teaches numbers 0 to 100 but doesn't.** `grammarFocusEnglish` promises "French numbers 0 to 100". The teaching text only covers the tens (vingt, trente, soixante, soixante-dix, quatre-vingts, quatre-vingt-dix). There is nothing on 1–16, the 17–19 pattern (dix-sept), or the et un forms (vingt et un, soixante et onze). The objective is "Count", and the lesson can't deliver that. Fix one of two ways:
- add a short teaching line on 1–16, dix-sept to dix-neuf, and the et un / et onze pattern, or
- narrow the focus to "the numbers used in this lesson" and confirm that a number table exists somewhere else in the app.
