# Correction proposals: foundation-roofing 5 and 6 (fr-en)

These are proposals only: nothing was edited and nothing here is an approval. I reviewed the pasted candidates only and did not open the source packets or verify their hashes.

## Lesson 5

1. **Objective and grammar teach held content.** "What you can do" and "Can + base verb… Can you help me?" rest only on card 10 and line 5, both held, and no row shows *can* for ability.
   - Proposed objective: "Talk about plans and the order of actions on the job."
   - Move the *can* sentence behind the register hold.

2. **Internal note in the grammar explanation.** Remove "source Spanish conjugations are not learning targets"; it means nothing to a French-speaking learner.

3. **`It's hot (weather).` used as target text.** The gloss sits in the hear activity target, form 2 (form and example), the wordbank and the dictionary headword, so it would be recorded or scored literally.
   - Proposed: target `It's hot.`, with "(weather)" moved to a separate sense note.

4. **"Be going to" collides with line 1.** "Today we're going to a new house" is movement (*go* in the present continuous), not *be going to + verb*, and French "on va" covers both.
   - Proposed addition: "We're going to a house = movement; we're going to start = plan."
   - Use line 7 as the model plan sentence, and the contraction "I'm" to match the cards.

5. **Line 1 "a new house" is ambiguous and the French is awkward.** It could be "une nouvelle maison" (a different one) or "une maison neuve" (newly built), and "on va à une nouvelle maison" is unidiomatic.
   - Proposed: hold the line pending a sense check against the source; then, for example, "on va sur une nouvelle maison / dans une autre maison".

6. **Write activity "On commence." → "Let's start." is under-determined.** "We're starting" and "We start" are equally correct answers.
   - Proposed: add a cue such as "(proposition à l'équipe)", or list accepted alternates before scoring.

7. **Speaking block contradictions.**
   - The role "Crew member: the person you ask for help" conflicts with the goal "help-request register remains held". Proposed: "Crew member: listens to your plan."
   - Provenance says "source closure", but the closure is marked newly authored with no anchors. Proposed: "mixed: newly-authored opening and closure, source wordbank."
   - Block-level anchors list `line:7`, which is unused, and omit `line:2`, which the opening uses.

8. **Form 3 "we do" / "on fait".** Since *faire* maps to both *do* and *make*, add "here do, not make". "Do is used in do the cleanup" explains nothing as written.

9. **See and recall prompts are the same task.** Both ask the learner to produce the English word. If "see" is meant as exposure or recognition, it should show the English with its French meaning. This applies to lesson 6 as well.

10. **Hear prompt wording.** Proposed: "Écoutez la phrase en anglais, puis donnez-en le sens en français."

## Lesson 6

1. **Objective "with regular verbs" is a Spanish-source artifact.** The English examples (*go*, *get*, *eat*, *cut*, *bring*) are irregular verbs.
   - Proposed: "Say what you and the crew do every day (present simple)."

2. **Grammar sentence "A singular crew takes gets in this source."** It is unclear and leaks source wording.
   - Proposed: "With he, she, it or a singular noun, add -s: The crew gets to the jobsite."

3. **Card 11 "Where do you live?" → "Vous habitez où ?" is not held.** It raises the same tu/vous question that holds lesson 5 card 10 and line 5.
   - Proposed: set the candidate row and dictionary 11 to held, and decide them with the same register decision.

4. **Card 1 "the work, the job" → "le travail" groups two senses but is not held.** Lesson 5 card 4 ("after, later") was held for the same reason. The see activity also asks for "le mot" while expecting a comma-joined pair.
   - Proposed: hold or split the card and dictionary 1, with a single target such as "work" or "the job".

5. **Unnatural English targets.** "the lunch" (card 8) and "the work" carry an article English does not use here, and "I nail" (line 4) lacks an object. The existing holds address only the French side.
   - Proposed: add an English-target check to those holds ("lunch", "work", "I nail them down").

6. **Speaking block provenance and anchors.**
   - Provenance says "source opening and closure", but the closure is newly authored with no anchors.
   - Block-level anchors omit card 12 and line 7, although the goal includes end-of-day cleanup.

7. **Type activity alternates.** "The crew arrives at the jobsite…" is a correct answer; list it before scoring is enabled.

8. **Card 5 "le collègue".** Proposed: "le/la collègue".
