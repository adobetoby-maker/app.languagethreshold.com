# Critique: Foundation Roofing 7 and 8 (fr-en)

Direction is correct in both lessons: the prompts are in French, the answers are in English, and every scored row is either unheld or exact source text. The corrections below are listed by priority.

## Lesson 7 (`foundation-roofing:7:fr-en`)

**Held-row leakage**
1. **The speaking word bank includes `when` and `who`.** The only source questions with *when* and *who* are held (card 10/line 4 *material*, card 11/line 2 *boss*). With an open partner, these words invite the held rows. Either drop both words, or give each one an unheld target.
2. **The goal adds "Who is there?"** That sentence is a door-knock formula, not jobsite speech. Its natural answers also point toward the held *boss* role. Remove it from the speaking goal and keep it only as the form 1 example.
3. **The goal text contains internal authoring language.** The phrase "as an explicitly new question-word example" is a provenance note, not something a learner should see. The quoted question also has no quotation marks. Rewrite the goal as: *"Ask where the ladder is and why work is stopping at eleven."*
4. **The learner role "A new crew member" comes from held line 1** ("Today is my first day"). The risk is low because the hold concerns the French side. Still, "A crew member" removes the dependency.
5. **The partner role says the colleague "answers your questions,"** which means the answer to *why stopping at eleven* is unscripted. That opens the door to unreviewed safety, weather, or break content. Add a constraint so the partner answers only from the source or a reviewed reply.

**Grammar explanation**
6. **The rule "question word, then verb, then subject" does not fit the lesson's own examples.**
   - "Why are we stopping?" follows the pattern word + *be* + subject + *-ing* verb.
   - "Who is there?" uses *who* as the subject.
   
   Limit the rule to *be* as a main verb or helper. For example: *"Question word + is/are + subject: 'Where is the ladder?' 'Why are we stopping?'"*

**Consistency**
7. **Reader line 6** ("…how do I put on the harness?") is unheld. Lesson 8 holds harness rows for fall-protection review. Either align the hold or record why this question form is exempt.

## Lesson 8 (`foundation-roofing:8:fr-en`)

**Exact source versus derivation**
1. **The grammar example "I never work on a wet roof" is a derivation.** The source reads *"We never work on a wet roof."* (line 3). Use the exact source sentence.
2. **"It isn't ready yet" is also a derivation.** Use the exact line 1: *"The roof isn't ready yet."*
3. **The counterexample "I never don't work" is not a realistic error for French speakers.** Their typical double negative comes from *ne…jamais / ne…rien*. Use *"not 'We don't never work…'"* or *"not 'There isn't nothing…'"* instead.

**Provenance and metadata**
4. **Form 2 provenance reads "from the anchored question word."** This was carried over from lesson 7; *nobody* is not a question word. Change it to "Newly authored example from the anchored negative word."
5. **The reader 5 hold omits a dropped word.** Source *"a few more nails"* became archived *quelques clous*, which loses *more*. Add that loss to the hold reason. The card 8 hold has the same gap.
6. **The card 6 hold cites "the current Spanish note… un otro."** For fr-en, Spanish is provenance only. Restate the hold in French and English terms.

**French support**
7. **`personne` on its own is ambiguous.** It can mean *a person* as well as *nobody*. This affects the hear activity, form 2 `meaningFrench`, and dictionary 2. Use *personne (ne…)* or add a gloss such as *« aucune personne »*.

**Held-row leakage**
8. **The speaking word bank includes `nobody`.** In a roof-readiness scene, this strongly invites the held *"Nobody goes up without a harness."* (card 11/line 2). Drop it, or add a constraint that excludes harness content.
9. **The closure "Thank you, I understand now." assumes the partner clarified something.** That is the natural follow-up to held line 7 ("…can you speak more slowly?"). The risk is acceptable on the English side, but note that line 7's French register hold has not been resolved.
