# Critique: French Reader meaning-support alternatives, Master 13–15

Overall, the 18 alternatives restore source meaning well where the archived French had softened it. They keep the probable cause, 911, "don't move", "kill", "hospital", the no-punishment promise, the no-slide claim, the lightning/thunder split and the female homeowner. Below are only concrete corrections. Items marked **(error)** should be fixed. The rest are wording or note fixes.

## Cross-cutting

1. **Coverage gap in rows the decision questions name.** The scope says "complete faithful French meaning support", but only 6 of 16/17/17 rows have alternatives. Several uncovered rows are exactly where the archived French softened the source:
   - **M13 r1.** "a las diez y cuarto" became "vers dix heures quinze", an exact time turned approximate. DQ3 names this.
   - **M13 r6.** "no te muevas" was dropped.
   - **M13 r9.** "a las diez y media" became "vers…", and "paramédicos" became "secouristes". DQ3 names the 10:30 time.
   - **M13 r16.** The crew action was replaced by "personne qualifiée".
   - **M14 r16.** The cones/sidewalk sign proposal was replaced. DQ1 names it.
   - **M15 r6, r7, r9, r12, r13, r17.** Close-up now, tarp now, loose material, "Ya tronó", "la lona ya está fija", and the 7 o'clock return were all rewritten. DQ1 names them.
   - **M15 r1, r11, r15.** "A la una y media", "a las dos menos diez" and "a las dos en punto" were approximated or lost "sharp".

   Either add alternatives for these rows or change `scope` so it no longer says "complete".
2. **English glosses inside quoted speech.** Inserts like "(roof bracket)", "(toe board)" and "(pickup)" appear inside « » quotes, which changes the speaker's exact face. Glossing is also inconsistent: lanyard, ridge and battens are glossed, but bâche/tarp, échelle/ladder and paramédics are not. Pick one approach. Either keep glosses outside the quote and in a separate field, or gloss every target technical term.
3. **Typography is mixed.** There is a space before `:`, `?` and `!` but none before `;`. That matches neither France (space before `;` too) nor Québec/OQLF (no space before `; ? !`). Choose a locale convention. Apostrophes are straight while the archive uses curly ones. That is fine given the normalization flag, but it should be stated.
