# Critique: M16–M18 French reader alternatives

I reviewed only the text you pasted. I did not open or hash any repo files, and nothing below approves, selects or completes anything. Rows not listed under a lesson have no corrections.

## Cross-cutting

1. **Inconsistent typography.** The rows mix two conventions: a space before `:` and `?` (France style), but no space before `;` (Quebec style). Examples: M16:15 `Très bien; si`, M18:4 `demain; serait-il … jeudi ? »`. Pick one locale and apply it everywhere. For France, put a narrow no-break space before `; : ? !`. For Quebec, put a space only before `:`. Record this as a locale note, not inside the utterance.
2. **Mixed apostrophes.** Candidates use `'` while archived text uses `’`. The normalization flag covers comparison, but choose one form for any future selected text.

## M16

**:3** `se trouvaient dans la fenêtre du garage` is a calque. Use **`se trouvaient à la fenêtre du garage, comme l'exige la ville.`** Don't change it to `affichés`, because that adds a posting act the source doesn't state.

**:7**
- `la pose des clous` is a generic paraphrase. The direct term is **`le clouage`**.
- `ajusté à la pente et au vent` should be **`en fonction de la pente et du vent`**.
- The alignment note claims passive-se is mirrored, but only the first clause does that. If mirroring matters, use `s'ajuste`.
- Proposed: **`Rogelio : « Ici, le clouage se fait selon les instructions du produit, et le nombre de clous s'ajuste en fonction de la pente et du vent, selon le fabricant. »`**

**:11** In the alignment note, delete "without adding an objective completion." It is meaningless for this row. Say instead: "'manque' keeps the absence claim on two courses as the inspector's statement."

**:13**
- The comma in `sur chaque rang, près du mur` loosens the restriction. The source restricts the requirement to courses next to the wall. Remove the comma.
- `a noté` should be `a inscrit`, to match "wrote … on the notice."
- The quoted finding should get guillemets, like the other frames.
- Proposed: **`L'inspecteur a inscrit une observation sur l'avis de correction : « Un solin à gradins doit être installé à chaque rang adjacent au mur. »`**
- The note "passive-se … is not automatically a valid objective in French" is garbled. Replace it with: "Spanish 'se debe instalar' is passive-se. French uses 'doit être installé'. Whether this serves the passive-se teaching point is a reviewer decision. The requirement is the inspector's written claim, not verified code."

**:15** In the note, "future-conditional 'vous aurez'" mislabels the form. It is a simple future in the result clause of a real condition. Say: "si + present → future kept. Approval remains conditional on confirmation."

## M17

**:6**

- `complétés` is an anglicism. Use `achevés`.
- `en plus` is colloquial. The formal report connector for *además* / "in addition" is **`de plus`**.
- The alignment note says "the source's 'en plus' link," but the source has *además*. Correct that.
- Proposed: **`Résumé : 14 carrés ont été achevés du côté est et, de plus, la noue principale a été terminée.`**
- Move `carrés de couverture` into the locale note, as a question about whether the qualifier is needed.

**:7**
- `support de toiture` is a generic stand-in for deck. Use **`platelage`**, and note the locale alternatives `pontage` and `support de couverture`.
- The note offers `panneau de sous-toiture` as a deck term. That is wrong: sous-toiture is underlayment. Delete it.
- Add a locale note for skylight: `fenêtre de toit` is France usage, while `puits de lumière` or `lanterneau` is Quebec usage.
- Proposed: **`Pendant la dépose, du platelage pourri a été trouvé près de la fenêtre de toit.`**

**:8**

- Keep `ordre de changement` (Quebec) vs. `avenant` (France) in the note only.

**:10** The current syntax breaks "before and after" away from "each replaced sheet." It also moves the attachment from the report to the file. Proposed: **`Sont jointes des photos avant et après de la réparation du platelage et de chaque panneau remplacé, comme preuve photographique pour le dossier.`**

## M18

**:4** The note says "No new date is assumed," but Thursday is proposed in this very row. Replace with: "Thursday is posed as a question, not asserted."

**:5** `vers huit heures` adds approximation. The source says "at eight." Use **`à huit heures`**: **`Message : « Nous pourrions arriver à huit heures, si cela vous convient, et les matériaux arriveraient à l'entrée du garage mercredi. »`**

**:9**
- The alignment note is wrong. "On Thursday we're not home" is already future in meaning, so the archived `serons` is not a tense shift. The real drift is `absents` (broader) vs. "not home."
- Delete "No address is supplied." It is irrelevant to this row.
- Optional: `Ici Daniel Kim, le mari` reads more naturally than `à l'appareil, le mari`.

**:12**
- The note says Rogelio's pronoun is not stated. That is false: the English target says "**he** got the voicemail," and other lessons use he/his. Remove both gender-avoidance justifications.
- `a obtenu la messagerie vocale` is a calque. The idiom is "tomber sur."
- Proposed: **`L'appel a été coupé, et Rogelio a recomposé le numéro de téléphone, mais il est tombé sur la messagerie vocale.`**
- Locale note: `boîte vocale` in Quebec.

**:13** The note's "proposed date" reframes the source. Use: "Conditional 'would be Friday at eight' kept. It is distinct from the indicative 'I confirm … 8:00 a.m.' in :15."

**:15**
- `Cette nuit-là` implies late night. "That night" after a day's work is **`Ce soir-là`**.
- `un préavis` imports a contractual notice-period sense. Use the direct form **`je vous préviendrai à l'avance`**.
- Proposed: **`Ce soir-là, Rogelio a écrit : « Je confirme le changement de date pour vendredi à 8 h 00; je vous préviendrai à l'avance si l'heure d'arrivée change. »`**
