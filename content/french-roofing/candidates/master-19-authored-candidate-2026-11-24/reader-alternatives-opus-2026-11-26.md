One term reads differently by locale: *déjeuner* means breakfast in Québec and lunch in France. Either choose neutral wording or flag each of these terms for locale review. Alignment notes must not present them as settled.
- **The source objectives stay unadapted, which is correct.** M20 still says "in Spanish" and M19 still names *siempre que / a menos que*. No row claims to have adapted or completed the objective.
