These are **draft source files**, not a working or released French course. The three `foundation-*-draft.json` files together contain lessons 1–10 for the stable `travel-breakfast` French→English pair. They preserve the exact English titles/objectives, 152 term and phrase anchors, and 89 aligned reading lines from the Spanish Travel Breakfast source for traceability.
