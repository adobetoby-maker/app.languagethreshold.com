These are editorial risk reductions, **not qualified allergy or food-service approval**. The auto-injector/emergency language in Master 4, the product and cross-contact implications in both lessons, the distinction between vegan choice, lactose avoidance and milk allergy, and the Spanish/French naturalness all require qualified review before registration or release.
