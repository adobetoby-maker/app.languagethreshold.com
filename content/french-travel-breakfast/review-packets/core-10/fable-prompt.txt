## Blocking

**1. `speaking` (whole block) does not belong to this lesson.**
- The roles, opening and all five objectives describe a family ordering task: wife, son, mother-in-law, "sans" for "without cheese", and asking for the check.
- None of that appears in the source passage or the cards. The scene here is a counter purchase with a receipt already in hand, so asking for the check also contradicts it.
- The opening "Qu'est-ce que je vous sers ?" is an order-taking line, not a correction line.
- It also assumes the learner has a wife and a mother-in-law.
- The `internalNotes[2]` justification cites a "speaking specification" that is not in the pinned source.
- **Correction:** rebuild the block around the correction scene.
  - Opening from the server: « Voici votre café au lait. »
  - Objectives: open with Excusez-moi + je crois que; contrast the drinks with pas; say il me manque; present voici mon reçu; ask with Pourriez-vous me l'échanger.
  - Remove the family members, sans and l'addition.
  - Remove or rewrite `internalNotes[2]`.

**2. `titleEnglish` is in French.** It currently reads "Corriger une commande". **Correction:** use "A wrong order" or "Correcting an order".

**3. `reader[2]` (line 3) and `forms[1].exampleFrench`: "mon sac" breaks the scene.**
- In French, "mon sac" reads as the customer's own handbag or backpack, so checking it for a pastry they just bought is illogical.
- The source "mi bolsa" here means the take-out bag.
- **Correction:** use « Je vérifie le sachet et je vois qu'il n'y a pas de viennoiserie (dedans). » Make the same change in `forms[1].exampleFrench`.
- The English support line stays exact to the source.

**4. `forms[1].explanationEnglish` misstates the rule.**
- It says "regular -ier verb, so nous takes -ions". That is misleading.
- Vérifier is a regular -er verb: stem vérifi- plus -ons gives vérifions. There is no separate -ier ending.
- **Correction:** "Vérifier is a regular -er verb; the stem vérifi- plus -ons gives nous vérifions."

**5. `forms[*].forms` has no vous row.**
- The source keys `él_ella_usted` and `ellos_ustedes` carry the polite usted forms.

- **Correction:** add a `"vous"` key to all five verbs: vous commandez, vous vérifiez, vous changez, vous apportez, vous dites.
- Add one sentence noting that Spanish usted is third person while French vous is second-person plural.

**6. `forms[1].exampleFrench` has an extra comma.** "mon sac, et il n'y a" should drop the comma, combined with the fix in #3: « Je vérifie le sachet et il n'y a pas de viennoiserie. »
