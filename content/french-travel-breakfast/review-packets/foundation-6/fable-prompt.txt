- **Card 13:** "Vous habitez ici ?" is correct for usted.

## 3. Grammar and naturalness

- **Card 16:** "Vous ouvrez les dimanches ?" is unnatural. Use **Vous êtes ouverts le dimanche ?**, with singular *le* for a habitual day.
- **Card 11 and Reader 2:** Acceptable. *Je prends mon petit-déjeuner* is slightly more natural. Do not use *déjeuner*, which means lunch in fr-FR.
- **Colons:** Use a non-breaking space before ":" and "?".

- **Form 3 (Reader 7):** After the tu fix, it becomes the **tu habites** form.
- **Coverage gap:** No usted/vous form appears. Add card 13 "Vous habitez ici ?" as the vous example.
- **No paradigm:** The source `verbs` block (four full paradigms with notes) has no French counterpart. Add paradigms for **manger** (note *mangeons*), **habiter**, and the irregular **prendre** and **boire** as lexical items.
- **Source note 7 is false.** French does have regular -ir verbs (2nd group: *finir*, *choisir*). The accurate statement is that Spanish conjugation classes don't map onto French ones, and *vivre* is irregular, so *habiter* is used.
