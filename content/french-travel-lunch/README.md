# French→English Lunch & Casual Dining

Foundation 1–10 are private source-mapped lessons in `foundation-1-3-draft.json` and `foundation-4-10-draft.json`. They follow the Spanish Lunch learning purposes while teaching French forms directly. Lessons 1–3 cover greetings, pronunciation, quantity and food/place description. Lessons 4–10 cover *avoir*, *il y a*, lunch plans and permission, present-tense subjects, questions, negation and food restrictions, polite requests and menu cognates. Lesson 2 uses euros and counted lunch items. Lesson 3 teaches French *être*, availability and location expressions without importing the Spanish *ser/estar* contrast as French grammar. Stable IDs use `foundation-travel-lunch:{n}:fr-en` and do not overlap the Spanish or Japanese progress identities.

The ten lessons have 159 contextual French/English cards, 83 aligned Reader turns, specific speaking scenes and 60 card-backed See, Recall, Hear, Type, Say and Write prompts. The [source validator](verify-private-foundation.py) checks the pinned Spanish Foundation file's hash, exact English learning purposes, stage-local IDs, card/Reader/speaking ownership, six distinct prompts per lesson and closed release flags. Run `python3 content/french-travel-lunch/verify-private-foundation.py` from the app checkout.

Core 1–30 are private source-mapped lessons in the six `core-*-draft.json` batches. The first twenty cover everyday lunch choices and problems, direct dietary questions, access and an itemized bill dispute. Lessons 21–25 cover declining an upsell, asking about local lunch customs, ordering a picnic by quantity, requesting emergency help during an allergic reaction and recovering a forgotten bag. Lessons 26–30 cover hosting a supplier, phone pickup for four, a communal table, leaving after a long wait and an extended group lunch with a child, severe shellfish allergy and contested charge. Together they have 451 cards, 268 aligned Reader turns, thirty speaking scenes and 180 card-backed practice prompts. The [Core validator](verify-private-core.py) pins `src/data/travel-zone.ts`, checks its 30-lesson shape and all thirty learning purposes, and verifies stable pair IDs and closed release flags. Run `python3 content/french-travel-lunch/verify-private-core.py` from the app checkout.

Master 1–15 are private source-mapped lessons in the three `master-*-draft.json` batches. The first five preserve the Spanish Master purposes of explaining a fixed-price lunch and checking understanding, explaining local hours and after-meal conversation, coaching an order at a busy counter, reading an allergen chart for a group, and resolving a vegetarian soup made with chicken stock. The next five compare substitute dishes for six, resolve an exact-price bill gap, discuss a food-poisoning concern, give specific feedback to an owner, and mediate a group lunch decision. Lessons 11–15 encourage a hungry group after kitchens close, switch between French *vous* and *tu* at a family table, report a burn to a manager, discuss a peanut-allergen near miss with direct epinephrine vocabulary, and alert a vendor and emergency operator to a gas smell. French scenes teach their own connectors, conditional phrasing, imperatives with object pronouns, allergen questions, past narration and reported speech. These fifteen lessons have 252 cards, 148 aligned Reader turns, fifteen speaking scenes and 90 authored mode prompts. The [Master validator](verify-private-master.py) pins the twenty-lesson Spanish source file, checks exact English purposes, stage-local IDs, card/Reader/speaking ownership, all six modes and closed release gates. Run `python3 content/french-travel-lunch/verify-private-master.py` from the app checkout.
