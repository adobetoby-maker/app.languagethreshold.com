# French→English Lunch & Casual Dining

Foundation 1–10 are private source-mapped lessons in `foundation-1-3-draft.json` and `foundation-4-10-draft.json`. They follow the Spanish Lunch learning purposes while teaching French forms directly. Lessons 1–3 cover greetings, pronunciation, quantity and food/place description. Lessons 4–10 cover *avoir*, *il y a*, lunch plans and permission, present-tense subjects, questions, negation and food restrictions, polite requests and menu cognates. Lesson 2 uses euros and counted lunch items. Lesson 3 teaches French *être*, availability and location expressions without importing the Spanish *ser/estar* contrast as French grammar. Stable IDs use `foundation-travel-lunch:{n}:fr-en` and do not overlap the Spanish or Japanese progress identities.

The ten lessons have 159 contextual French/English cards, 83 aligned Reader turns, specific speaking scenes and 60 card-backed See, Recall, Hear, Type, Say and Write prompts. The [source validator](verify-private-foundation.py) checks the pinned Spanish Foundation file's hash, exact English learning purposes, stage-local IDs, card/Reader/speaking ownership, six distinct prompts per lesson and closed release flags. Run `python3 content/french-travel-lunch/verify-private-foundation.py` from the app checkout.

Core 1–25 are private source-mapped lessons in the five `core-*-draft.json` batches. The first twenty cover everyday lunch choices and problems, direct dietary questions, access and an itemized bill dispute. The next five cover declining an upsell, asking about local lunch customs, ordering a picnic by quantity, requesting emergency help during an allergic reaction and recovering a forgotten bag. Together they have 375 cards, 218 aligned Reader turns, twenty-five speaking scenes and 150 card-backed practice prompts. The [Core validator](verify-private-core.py) pins `src/data/travel-zone.ts`, checks its 30-lesson shape and the first twenty-five learning purposes, and verifies stable pair IDs and closed release flags. Run `python3 content/french-travel-lunch/verify-private-core.py` from the app checkout.
