# Japanese Foreman Foundation 1–3 private pilot

This pilot is an unpublished Japanese→English draft based on the current English objectives, vocabulary, phrases, and stage-local lesson numbers in `src/data/core60/es/foundation-construction-foreman.json`. The pinned English source and SHA-256 are in `foundation-source-1-3.json`. `foundation-runtime-pilot-1-3.json` contains Japanese wording and original aligned readings. `reviewed:false` means no qualified human Japanese or construction review has occurred.

Run `python3 scripts/validate-japanese-foreman-foundation-pilot.py` to check the exact English source hash and every English word/phrase, aligned readings, cloze reconstruction, and review flag. `tests/japanese-foreman-pilot-gate.test.mjs` asserts that Japanese users cannot be routed into the existing Spanish Foundation stage.

**Runtime gate:** the app's Core 60 stage manifest and curriculum registry resolve by `moduleId` alone. `foundation-construction-foreman` currently resolves to the Spanish stage at `src/data/core60/es/foundation-construction-foreman.json`; the curriculum registry caches by that same ID. A Japanese file with the same ID would either shadow Spanish or remain unreachable. `getCurriculum(moduleId)` and app lesson callers also lack the target/native pair. A safe shared-ID solution requires `(moduleId, target, native)` keys throughout stage lookup, cache, curriculum, route loading and progress review, plus tests that both pairs retain their own content and progress. The alternative is distinct language-specific module/stage IDs with an explicit progress migration plan. Neither change fits this three-lesson data pilot. The stage validator also requires all ten Foundation lessons, so three cannot be registered as a full stage.

Do not expose this pilot as a complete course, merge it as a released track, or attach Spanish audio. Open gates: independent AI rechecks and fixes, Foundation 4–10, full stage/runtime mapping, six-mode and Reader/card/speaking integration, Japanese dictionary and verbs, recorded ja-JP audio with listening review, phone/offline/native acceptance, qualified human and trade review.
