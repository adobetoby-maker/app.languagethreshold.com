# Japanese→English Roofing Supervisor draft

Foundation 1–10, Core 1–30 and Master 1–11 are private authored drafts for a Japanese equivalent of the Spanish Roofing course. The previous Japanese program coverage snapshot did not include Roofing among its 56 catalog-compatible tracks; this is a newly proposed Spanish-parity analogue, not one of the originally counted 55 remaining tracks.

The English objectives and progression are pinned to the Spanish Roofing curriculum and its English Reader translations. The Japanese passages and grammar are adapted for Japanese, with stable `foundation-roofing:N:ja-en`, `roofing:N:ja-en` and `master-roofing:N:ja-en` lesson IDs. The source objective for Foundation 3 names Spanish *ser* and *estar* only as provenance; the learner-facing lesson teaches Japanese identity and condition constructions. Core 30 adapts the source objective “in Spanish” to Japanese while retaining that wording only as source provenance. Core 1–30 preserve all 440 English card anchors, 251 aligned Reader lines, and 150 English verb-example anchors. Master 1–11 add 231 card anchors, 177 aligned Reader lines and 55 English verb-example anchors; the Japanese examples are authored drafts.

All content is `reviewed:false`, `registered:false`, with no audio.
