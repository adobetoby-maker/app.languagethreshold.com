# Japanese→English Roofing Supervisor draft

Foundation 1–10 are private authored drafts for a Japanese equivalent of the Spanish Roofing course. The previous Japanese program coverage snapshot did not include Roofing among its 56 catalog-compatible tracks; this is a newly proposed Spanish-parity analogue, not one of the originally counted 55 remaining tracks.

The English objectives and progression are pinned to the Spanish Roofing curriculum and its English Reader translations. The Japanese passages and grammar are adapted for Japanese, with stable `foundation-roofing:N:ja-en` lesson IDs. The source objective for lesson 3 names Spanish *ser* and *estar* only as provenance; the learner-facing lesson teaches Japanese identity and condition constructions.

All content is `reviewed:false`, `registered:false`, with no audio. A fluent Japanese reviewer should check natural construction terminology, especially シングル材, フルハーネス, and measurement phrasing. A qualified roofing reviewer should check technical claims separately, including the English F5 “work fast because it is hot” line and F8's absolute wet-roof statement. F10 keeps 911 in its U.S.-site context; the incident wording needs contextual review. These are internal review notes, not learner-facing cautions. Before release, complete Core 1–30, Master 1–20, six working modes, Reader, cards, speaking, dictionary senses, offline behavior, recorded Japanese audio, and web/native acceptance.

Run `python3 scripts/validate-japanese-roofing-foundation-drafts.py` to check source hashes, IDs, pairs, and release gates.
