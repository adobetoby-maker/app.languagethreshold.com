# Japanese Breakfast & Cafés — Foundation draft

Exact pair: Japanese target / English support. Stable base module `travel-breakfast`; stage `foundation-travel-breakfast`. Lessons 1–10 are original café scenes anchored to the existing English objectives in `src/data/core60/es/foundation-travel-breakfast.json`. The full source file and each lesson's objective, speaking prompt, and English word anchors are SHA-256 pinned in `foundation-1-10-draft.json` and checked by `scripts/validate-japanese-travel-breakfast-draft.py`.

The source's Spanish pronunciation, peso prices and ser/estar distinction cannot transfer directly. These Japanese drafts introduce kana and long vowels, cup/item counters and yen, and predicate adjectives/current-state expressions instead. Fictional prices are only examples. Each lesson includes aligned Reader turns, cards, a speaking mission and content inputs for See, Recall, Hear, Type, Say and Write. These are **private draft inputs**, not registered runtime lessons or verified six-mode implementations.

The [Core 1–3 draft](core-1-3-draft.json) pins the exact English lesson objectives and roleplays from `src/data/travel-zone.ts`, plus all English word anchors and Reader lines from `public/lesson-content/es/travel-breakfast.json`. Original Japanese café scenes have their own aligned English support; the Spanish/Roman/Mexican setting is provenance, not learner copy. The cumulative validator checks both stages, stable IDs, source hashes, pairs, Reader, speaking and six-mode content inputs.
