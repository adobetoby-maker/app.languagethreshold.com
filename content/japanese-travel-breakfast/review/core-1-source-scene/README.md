# Japanese Breakfast Core 1 source-scene packet

`source-setting-candidate.json` is an inert Japanese→English review packet for Core 1. It pins the current Breakfast plan, Spanish lesson cards and Reader, and archived Japanese draft. Run `python3 build.py --check` and `python3 validate.py` here for source hashes, exact card/Reader order, stage-local identity, revision-scoped text-hashed candidate rows, retired archived positions, and closed gates.

The 15 cards and nine Reader turns follow the current source's crowded café, counter, waitress, patio table, coffee and *pan dulce*. The archived Japanese scene instead names Sam, a station café and a window seat; those rows remain archived, with no saved-item migration. The plan says “Roman café,” while the Spanish Reader names no city and its *pan dulce* note is Mexican/US. The candidate Reader names no city, preserves *pan dulce* as a source term and leaves the plan/Reader locale decision visible. Japanese `カフェ` carries the shop sense here, while Spanish `café` also means the drink; `コーヒー` appears in Reader 9.

Cards 11–14 are traveler-facing questions. Card 15 is a staff-side polite question; the source note also permits a traveler check, whose Japanese wording still needs a separate variant. All Reader turns contain narrator framing; quoted speakers are separately labeled so narrated audio is not automatically assigned a character voice. Pin `空いている` to `あいている` (vacant) before audio. Japanese readings, six authored mode prompts/answers, contextual forms, full speaking setup, dictionary senses, saved-item policy and qualified Japanese/food/locale/register review remain open. Source text itself is `reviewed:false`.

The 24 new candidate IDs are not runtime or audio IDs. They carry text hashes, and any change requires a new revision before promotion. Four of 24 candidate Japanese texts exactly overlap the archived draft, but those old rows have no candidate placement IDs and no audio identity transfers. A fresh two-voice scope would be 48 placement clips, 768 submitted Japanese characters and about $0.02304 gross at $30 per million characters; synthesis is held for the source and qualified-review decisions. Every approved clip would need its own technical and listening decision. All review, learner, scoring, audio/listening, offline, progress, owner-route and V4 gates remain false. AI assistance and structural checks are not qualified approval.
