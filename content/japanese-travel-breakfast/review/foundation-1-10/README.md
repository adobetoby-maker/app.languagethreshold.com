# Japanese Breakfast Foundation 1–10 source-objective audit

`source-objective-audit.json` is an inert, reproducible comparison of the archived Japanese Foundation draft against the current Spanish Breakfast Foundation curriculum and Reader. `build-source-audit.py --check` pins the source and draft file hashes, verifies ten ordered stage-local lesson IDs, exact English objective anchors, the six draft mode labels, and closed review/registration/audio state. It records the source and Japanese draft English card and Reader text for direct repository review.

The 120 Japanese-draft cards have six exact English strings matching a source card in the same lesson; none of the 89 Japanese-draft Reader turns exactly matches a source English turn. This **strict text overlap is not a semantic parity verdict**: the Japanese draft intentionally uses original scenes, local foods, yen and Japanese counters rather than literal Spanish dialogue. The objectives are visibly linked lesson for lesson, but a qualified reviewer must decide whether the Japanese cards, Reader, speaking and forms teach each source objective adequately and whether the changed scenes preserve specialist, food and service meaning. The draft's six `modes` are prose descriptions, not six authored prompt/answer activities.

Source-grounded review questions: Foundation 2's Spanish objective includes clock time, but the Japanese Reader uses only quantities and price while clock time appears in cards. Foundation 8's source Reader explicitly states a peanut allergy, but the Japanese Reader covers meat avoidance and omits the allergy statement; its allergy and nuts cards do not teach that claim in context. Foundation 9's source tip becomes a card-payment scene, a locale choice needing review. Foundation 4's fresh orange juice becomes freshly squeezed juice, which changes the food claim. A safe-mode Claude CLI read-only audit reported `modelUsage.canonicalModel` `claude-haiku-5-5` and raised these questions from the generated audit; it could not open the three pinned source files. We checked each question against those files ourselves. The AI output is not qualified Japanese or domain approval.

This audit does not rewrite candidate lessons or authorize a migration of saved cards, Reader positions, scoring or progress. It adds no owner page, learner import, audio asset, offline pack or V4 change. `reviewed:false`, registration, audio, scoring and progress gates remain closed. Qualified Japanese/food/locale review, contextual dictionary senses, six authored activities, exact-text audio with individual listening decisions, phone/offline and independent V4 acceptance remain open.
