# Japanese Breakfast Foundation 4 and 9 source-setting proposals

`source-correction-proposals.json` is an inert Japanese→English review packet for two archived Foundation scenes. Run `python3 build.py --check` and `python3 validate.py` to verify the current Spanish curriculum, Spanish Reader and archived Japanese draft by SHA-256, exact English source positions, lesson identity, candidate IDs and false gates.

Foundation 4 proposes a Japanese-language practice dialogue in the source's Oaxaca breakfast setting. Its Spanish curriculum note explicitly explains *jugo de naranja natural* as freshly squeezed; the source server answers yes. The archived Japanese original scene instead asks if the juice is freshly squeezed, receives no, then orders tea. The proposed source-setting Reader asks for fresh-squeezed juice and preserves the affirmative answer, fruit, toast, tea, utensils and register line. “Fresh” in the English source and the natural/freshly squeezed Spanish note are shown together for qualified language and locale review. The original negative availability contrast and kana-first card style require separate activity and script decisions.

Foundation 9 proposes a Japanese-language practice dialogue in the source's Oaxaca café setting, with cash payment of 200 pesos and the instruction to leave change as a tip. The archived Japanese original scene switches to card payment. Neither scene establishes a universal payment or tipping rule. The source gives no bill total or tip percentage.
