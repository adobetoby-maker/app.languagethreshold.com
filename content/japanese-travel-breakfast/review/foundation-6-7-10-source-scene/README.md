# Japanese Breakfast Foundation 6, 7 and 10 source-scene candidates

`source-setting-candidates.json` is an inert Japanese→English review packet. It pins the current Spanish curriculum, aligned Reader and archived Japanese draft by SHA-256. `python3 build.py --check` and `python3 validate.py` verify the exact source English/Spanish rows, source order, Japanese candidate presence, stable lesson IDs, revision-scoped card/Reader IDs, old-position retirement plans and false gates. Counts: Foundation 6 has 16 cards and nine Reader turns; Foundation 7 has 16 and nine; Foundation 10 has 15 and nine.

The archived Japanese scenes are original Sam/Japan variants. This packet follows the Spanish Breakfast source scenes for review: Emma and Lucía discuss Oaxaca/Puebla breakfast foods in Foundation 6; Emma and Tom question Rosa about an included hotel breakfast, café de olla, concha and the restroom in Foundation 7; Emma asks Rosa about chicken chilaquiles and a vegetarian option at Café Luna in Foundation 10. The archived scenes remain intact. All new candidate rows have revision-scoped IDs; old rows are retired **only from this candidate**. Archived rows lack explicit row IDs, so no saved-item migration, progress write or score mapping is authorized.

The packet also leaves visible questions that need a qualified Japanese, Mexican food, locale and dictionary decision. Foundation 6's Spanish `tú`/`usted` contrast maps imperfectly to Japanese register. Foundation 7's source says breakfast is included and ends at 11, without stating a 1,000-yen charge or specifying a room rate. Foundation 10's source teaches Spanish cognates and asks how to say something *in Spanish*; that does not directly become a Japanese learning objective. Its emergency information is written in English and Spanish on the wall, while its separate help card is a real emergency phrase. Japanese dialogue in the Mexican source setting is a parity proposal, not an established locale adaptation. The source's listed ingredients are not an allergen-safety claim.

These packets do not supply authored prompt/answer pairs for See, Recall, Hear, Type, Say or Write, contextual dictionary senses, a reviewed speaking card, recorded audio, offline assets or V4 integration. The candidate speaking goals are source-aligned review text only. All review, learner, scoring, listening, audio, offline, progress, owner-route and V4 gates remain false. Changed exact Japanese text needs a new audio scope, technical verification and distinct per-clip listening decisions if the lesson is approved for that step. No existing recording approval transfers to these candidate strings.
