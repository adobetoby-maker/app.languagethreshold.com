# Japanese Breakfast Foundation 6–7 audio candidates

These 100 `ja-JP` MP3s are inert, source-only review assets for the 50 card and Reader placements in [the Foundation 6–7 source scene candidate](../source-setting-candidates.json). Each exact placement has a separate female and male recording. `scope.json`, `manifest.json`, and `verification.json` pin source SHA-256, placement ID, Japanese text, voice, file hash, bytes, duration, and decoded signal. Run `python3 validate.py` with `ffmpeg` and `ffprobe` against the current source packet. This folder has no app, public playback route, owner page, native bundle, or offline pack import.

Synthesis used Google Cloud Text-to-Speech `ja-JP-Chirp3-HD-Aoede` and `ja-JP-Chirp3-HD-Charon` under quota project `project-91661021-fb2d-4524-b9f`. The submitted 1,448 characters have an estimated gross cost of $0.04344 at [the current $30/million-character Chirp 3 HD rate](https://cloud.google.com/text-to-speech/pricing); invoice and free-tier adjustments are unknown. Two clips had excess leading silence; the originals remain private and `signal-trims.json` pins the technical trims. The final 100-file decoded-signal verification has zero flags.

Open `listening-review.html` locally to assess **each exact clip separately**. Its pending controls do not write approval. All 100 clips retain `listeningReviewed:false`; reviewed, registration, learner, scoring, offline, progress, and release gates stay closed. Qualified Japanese, Mexican-food/locale, dictionary, and saved-item review remain open. A later source text or placement change invalidates that recording identity and its listening decision. Foundation 10 was intentionally excluded because its Spanish-cognate objective and language-help card remain unresolved for Japanese learners. English speaking-goal notes are review text only and were not synthesized.

The lesson text is pinned repository source and an unapproved Japanese candidate. Synthesis used named Google Cloud voices; this packet makes no claim of legal or listening approval. [Google Cloud service terms](https://cloud.google.com/terms/service-terms) are included for provenance.
