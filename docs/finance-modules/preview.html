Travel money: work the amount out, then say it

Example 1 · Count your change

Practice price: 145 pesos. Cash handed over: 200 pesos. Expected change: 55 pesos.

Le di 200 pesos. El precio era de 145. ¿Me faltan 5 pesos de cambio?

I gave you 200 pesos. The price was 145. Am I missing 5 pesos in change?

The example assumes the seller handed back 50. The learner states the facts, asks politely and recounts with the seller.

Example 2 · Confirm the net exchange amount

Fictional offer: 100 USD at 17 MXN per USD = 1,700 MXN before a 50 MXN fee. Net amount received: 1,650 MXN.

Entonces recibo 1,650 pesos después de la comisión, ¿correcto?

So I receive 1,650 pesos after the fee, correct?

These are invented practice figures, not a current exchange quote. All examples state the quote direction and the currency of each fee.

Course standard and review

  • Each target/native pair has its own grammar explanations, sentence-aligned reading and spoken role context.
  • Every lesson maps to See, Recall, Hear, Type, Say and Write, with an exact Reader passage and authored deck.
  • Master adds complications and clarification: a disputed charge, changed quote, missing record or competing explanation.
  • Distinguish financial concepts, amounts and outcomes. Avoid relabeling every advanced work task as a certified CEFR level.
  • Keep country-specific tax, deposit-protection and retirement-account examples labeled by jurisdiction. Teach terms in context rather than giving personalized product or tax recommendations.
  • Use existing exact-match HD recordings where available. New material uses online HD playback while prerecorded coverage remains incomplete. Device speech is disabled for these courses.
  • Track authored content, human terminology review, audio, offline readiness and production release separately. Model review is complete. Qualified human review, a full new recording batch and production release are separate.

Terminology details to get right

  • cuenta can mean an account or a restaurant bill; teach the context.
  • cambio can mean change returned or currency exchange; ask which amount is being discussed.
  • debe / haber are bookkeeping debit/credit labels; tarjeta de débito / crédito names card types, and crédito can refer to borrowing.
  • A card hold, final charge and refund are different events. Practice asking which one appears.
  • Use factura, recibo and comprobante according to the document and region rather than treating all three as identical.
