# Source shelf and checks

Original lesson prose and fictional figures were authored for the requested daily-work and money exchanges. These references informed terminology and checks; the courses do not reproduce their lessons or give live quotes.

- [CFPB adult financial education resources](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/educator-tools/adult-financial-education/tools-and-resources/): personal finance topic coverage.
- [IRS Publication 850](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p850), September 2023 glossary: US English/Spanish tax terms.
- [IRS Publication 505 (2026)](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p505): estimated-payment period distinctions in Accounting Core 24 and Master 7. The US April–May period differs from a calendar quarter.
- [IRS CP2000 guidance](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/understanding-your-cp2000-series-notice) and [Topic 652](https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc652): Accounting Master 5 distinguishes a proposed change from a bill; Codex corrected a blanket payment-delay implication because interest can continue and optional payment is possible.
- [Agencia Tributaria: pagos fraccionados](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/folleto-actividades-economicas/3-impuesto-sobre-renta-personas-fisicas/3_7-pagos-fraccionados.html): Spanish quarterly terminology and the July example in Accounting Master 7.
- [Agencia Tributaria: responding to notices](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/irpf/puedo-contestar-requerimiento-presentar-documentacion/contestar-requerimientos.html): documentation and response vocabulary. Lessons read the fictional notice's own deadline instead of asserting a universal response period.

- [SEC Investor.gov: avoiding investment scams](https://www.investor.gov/protect-your-investments/fraud/protect-your-money): high guaranteed returns with little or no risk are a warning sign; a written promise is not proof.
- [IRS retirement distribution rules](https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/plan-participant-employee/401k-resource-guide-plan-participants-general-distribution-rules): the general early-distribution distinction and exceptions, without asserting a universal outcome.

Primary-source spot checks made 15 November 2026 (America/Denver). They support the named distinctions and do not establish qualified human review of all 360 lessons. Model reports retain their original uncertainties; Codex corrections can supersede individual findings without rewriting the model's report.
