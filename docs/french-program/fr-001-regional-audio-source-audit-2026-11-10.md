The two “pain” files give a possible same-word nasal-vowel comparison with named regional descriptions. Their pages do **not** prove a phonetic contrast, recording comparability, or suitability for a scored listening question. The Paris “rue” sample is only one /ʁ/ candidate, not a two-speaker lesson. No candidate was downloaded, clipped, transcribed afresh, or placed in web/V4 assets.

Next evidence required: select at least two suitable recordings per target feature with speaker/region and exact-word provenance; inspect current file revision and license; complete a qualified French phonetic listening review of the actual waveform; clear any product redistribution and attribution obligations; then pin file hashes, transcript, offsets, and review status before writing Master 16–17 Hear tasks.
