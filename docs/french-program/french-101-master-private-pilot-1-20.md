# French 101 Master 1–20 private runtime fixture

This stacked draft completes the private French 101 Master stage fixture: 20 stable lesson keys, 400 newly authored bilingual card pairs, and 240 aligned Reader pairs. The deterministic builder pins redesigned drafts, support overlays, and legacy English-source hashes. All original Reader lines and cards remain in the sidecar. Each lesson also derives exact-pair dictionary entries from its selected term cards; these are private sidecar inputs, not registered dictionary entries. Lesson 20 has only five Reader lines within the shared twelve-word phrase limit, so its stage subset adds one short French/English excerpt taken verbatim from Reader line 5; the full line remains intact in Reader and cards.

The full 20-lesson Master stage passes structural and stage-word validators. Six-mode inputs are present, but the fixture remains `registered:false`, `reviewed:false`, and script-only without recorded audio. Independent French/editorial review, qualified human review, audio validation, and authenticated phone/offline acceptance are still required before release. The capstone is a practice scenario, not proof of spontaneous speaking proficiency. No live curriculum or learner progress is changed.
