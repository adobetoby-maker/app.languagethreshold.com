# French Lunch private dictionary inventory — 2026-11-10

The French→English Travel Lunch draft has 60 private lessons and 948 contextual card placements. `content/french-travel-lunch/build-private-dictionary-candidate.py` pins all twelve source-file SHA-256 values and derives 868 exact French/English/kind entries from those cards. It retains every card's stable lesson ID, index, source note, scene and any exact-substring Reader example. This is a review fixture: no sense is marked reviewed and none is registered in the learner dictionary.

The inventory has 839 normalized French headwords. **Twenty-five** have more than one exact English gloss and require contextual French review before any glosses are merged or selected. For example, `un café` appears as *a café* and *a coffee*; `le bouillon` has *broth*, *the broth* and *the stock / broth*; `la formule du midi` has *the fixed-price lunch* and *the set lunch*. These are queue entries, not AI resolutions. One exact pair, `en espèces` / *in cash*, is classified as both `term` and `phrase` in source cards and also awaits an authoring decision. Clinical and allergy vocabulary stays explicit in the source and candidate inventory.

A literal substring scan finds a matching Reader line for 480 of 948 card placements. The other 468 are listed with lesson context for review. Lack of a literal match does **not** prove the concept is absent from the Reader: inflection, synonyms and paraphrase can differ. It does mean the fixture cannot yet pin an exact Reader example for those placements. The qualified reviewer should resolve the 25 multi-gloss headwords and the classification conflict, then inspect the Reader gaps in context before the dictionary is wired to runtime.

Run `python3 content/french-travel-lunch/build-private-dictionary-candidate.py` to verify the pinned candidate against all twelve source files. `--write` regenerates it after an intentional source correction.
