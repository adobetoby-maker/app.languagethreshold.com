# Japanese 101 forms and kana private integration

This draft combines the 60-lesson contextual form candidate with Japanese-specific Kana Pad writing groups. The exact Japanese→English adapter `src/data/private-japanese101-form-candidate.ts` reads a supplied private form preview; no route or live lesson imports it. It verifies the current lesson definition, stage-local lesson identity, source objective, paired meaning/production activities, exact Reader turn, English cue, Japanese answer, and private review state before exposing a lesson's form prompts. A wrong language pair returns no prompts. The release constant remains `false`, and the released adapter returns `null`.

All 235 form prompts map to the current 60 lesson definitions and to Kana Pad group IDs for the kana signs in their answers. The focused test checks that every hiragana and katakana sign in the answer is represented by a writing group; kanji remain in the authored mixed-script answer and are not claimed as Kana Pad characters. The hiragana long-vowel group is not inferred merely from an ordinary う or い; long-vowel instruction needs its own authored context. Existing Spanish and Japanese curriculum objects, module IDs, and progress keys are unchanged. This is a source-backed integration candidate, not an accepted language lesson or learner-facing form activity.

Validation: `scripts/build-japanese-basics.py --check`, all six Japanese form builders with `--check`, focused form/Kana Pad/source-pair tests, `npm run typecheck`, and `git diff --check`. Fluent Japanese review of form naturalness, acceptable answer variants, kana instruction, and progression remains open, as do recorded-audio listening, runtime/offline/phone, and independent V4 acceptance. Keep `reviewed:false` and `registered:false` until those gates pass.

The private bridge now also pins the SHA-256 identities of all six form batches and rejects missing, changed, or extra source entries and malformed prompt IDs. A changed source must be rebuilt and deliberately rechecked against the current lesson definitions; it cannot pass merely because a replacement hash is well formed. Five focused form/Kana Pad tests, TypeScript and whitespace checks pass. Release remains disabled.
