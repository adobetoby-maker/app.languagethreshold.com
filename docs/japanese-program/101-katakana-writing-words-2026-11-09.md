# Japanese 101 katakana word writing — private review draft

The Japanese writing pad already exposes the ten basic katakana rows and 46 characters. Its Trace mode advanced directly from characters to a sentence because the katakana groups had no `PRACTICE_WORDS`. This draft adds two contextual writing words after each row, without changing row IDs, character order, lesson IDs, or learner progress keys. The writing components now remount when the learner chooses another row, so an in-progress character/word index cannot carry into a shorter row or another script. The guide and scorer now share a fitted font size for whole words; the former character-sized font could clip a four-character word inside the square canvas.

Thirteen of the 20 row/word placements reuse exact Japanese→English word pairs already present in the Japanese 101 Foundation/Core/Master lesson files: `アメリカ`/United States, `ドア`/door, `コピー`/copy, `クラス`/class, `ノート`/notebook, `メニュー`/menu, `メンバー`/member, `グループ`/group, `ペン`/pen, and `パン`/bread. The seven supplemental words are `スタッフ`/staff, `テスト`/test, `ニュース`/news, `ホテル`/hotel, `ハム`/ham, `ヨガ`/yoga, and `ユーロ`/euro. These fill rows where the current 60 lessons contain no suitable base katakana word; they are not represented as existing lesson vocabulary.

`tests/japanese-katakana-word-practice.test.mjs` checks every row has two words, each word contains a character from its row, source-backed pairs retain the exact lesson meaning, and the seven supplements are tracked separately. This is `reviewed:false` and remains in a draft PR until fluent Japanese review of the supplemental choices, romanization, handwriting difficulty, and placement. The words are not an extra completed lesson or evidence of full script/kanji progression. Audio, physical-device writing acceptance, and the rest of the French/Japanese parity gates remain open.
