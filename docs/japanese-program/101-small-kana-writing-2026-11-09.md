# Japanese 101 small-kana writing supplement

This private continuation follows the basic and voiced writing progression in draft PR #474. It appends two groups to each script, after all 15 existing groups: small `っ/ッ`, then contracted `ゃゅょ/ャュョ`. Existing group IDs, order, character rows, 60 lesson identities, and saved progress keys are unchanged. Each added group moves from character tracing to whole-word writing; the pad explains the small sign's role in the selected script. This is a Japanese-specific script supplement, not a renamed Spanish lesson.

The five added hiragana word placements are exact Japanese 101 card pairs: `きっぷ`/ticket, `ゆっくり`/slowly, `しゃしん`/photo, `しゅくだい`/homework, and `きょう`/today. Katakana adds one exact pair, `メニュー`/menu, plus four explicitly supplemental words: `チケット`/ticket, `スタッフ`/staff, `シャツ`/shirt, and `ショッピング`/shopping. The three contracted signs appear in contextual words in each script. `ショッピング` also provides a combined contracted-sound and small-ッ example. The source-pair test checks exact card meanings and ensures supplements are kept distinct.

Focused tests verify the original 46 basic and 25 voiced signs in each script remain in order, the four new small signs follow them, every new group has contextual word practice, source-card and supplement meanings are distinct, and the small-sign notes apply to the examples. At a 390×844 web viewport, the hiragana small-っ and contracted-sign notes and the katakana small-ッ note rendered with no page error or horizontal overflow. The writing pad still needs qualified Japanese review of word selection, romanization and teaching wording, plus native handwriting acceptance. These supplemental groups do not register a new course, approve audio, or change learner progress.
