# Japanese 101 small-kana writing supplement

This private continuation follows the basic and voiced writing progression in draft PR #474. It appends three groups to each script, after all 15 existing groups: small `っ/ッ`, contracted `ゃゅょ/ャュョ`, then long-vowel spelling (`う`/`い` in hiragana words and `ー` in katakana). Existing group IDs, order, character rows, 60 lesson identities, and saved progress keys are unchanged. Each added group moves from character tracing to whole-word writing; the pad explains the selected spelling pattern. This is a Japanese-specific script supplement, not a renamed Spanish lesson.

The eight added hiragana word placements are exact Japanese 101 card pairs: `きっぷ`/ticket, `ゆっくり`/slowly, `しゃしん`/photo, `しゅくだい`/homework, `きょう`/today, `きのう`/yesterday, `せんせい`/teacher, and `えいが`/movie. Katakana adds exact pairs `メニュー`/menu, `ノート`/notebook, and `コピー`/copy, plus four explicitly supplemental words: `チケット`/ticket, `スタッフ`/staff, `シャツ`/shirt, and `ショッピング`/shopping. The three contracted signs appear in contextual words in each script. `ショッピング` also provides a combined contracted-sound and small-ッ example. The source-pair test checks exact card meanings and ensures supplements are kept distinct.

Focused tests verify the original 46 basic and 25 voiced signs in each script remain in order, the small and long-vowel groups follow them, every new group has contextual word practice, and source-card and supplement meanings are distinct. At a 390×844 web viewport, the hiragana small-っ, contracted-sign, and long-vowel notes and the katakana small-ッ and long-vowel notes rendered with no page error or horizontal overflow. The writing pad still needs qualified Japanese review of word selection, romanization and teaching wording, plus native handwriting acceptance. These supplemental groups do not register a new course, approve audio, or change learner progress.
