# Japanese Dinner Foundation 4–6 private draft

Lessons 4–6 extend the Japanese→English Dinner course by the corresponding Spanish Foundation purposes. Lesson 4 asks whether a table exists, gives a reservation, names a needed chair, and asks about waiting. Lesson 5 says where the group will wait, marks an anniversary plan, asks permission for a toast, and asks whether the fish can be made without garlic. Lesson 6 uses polite Japanese non-past forms for eating, drinking, and sharing; it distinguishes broad 魚介類 (seafood) from 貝類 (shellfish) instead of treating them as the same word. The after-dinner conversation custom is expressed as 食後のおしゃべり, not a borrowed Spanish grammar term.
