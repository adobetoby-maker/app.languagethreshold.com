# Landscaper practice draft

October16,2026. Spanish target/English instruction only. Roofing remains the first release target. This expansion is unreviewed practice content, not a verified curriculum, trade qualification or safety procedure.

All30 existing lesson numbers and titles remain stable. Lesson1 retains its crew-meeting word list and reading. Lessons2–30 now have their own10terms,5phrases, aligned bilingual scenario and communication objective. The former repeated irrigation passage and universal field prescriptions were removed from the lesson instructions. Every new term and phrase appears in its own reading; this is usage coverage, not proof of naturalness or instructional quality.

The shared six-mode toolkit uses the lesson's own words, reading and scenario. Lessons1–10 begin with translation, four-item matching and one-sentence responses;11–20 use six-item matching, contextual listening and two-sentence responses;21–30 use eight-item matching, connected listening and three-sentence responses. Learners can still request support. Handwriting begins with copying, then recall and scenario responses. Each Say step retains its own lesson mission and communication goal. These stages are teaching settings, not assessed CEFR levels.

This public authored content avoids a first-open paid word-list build for this exact pair. A separate public Landscaper pack now includes all30 draft lessons and hashed app assets; it can coexist with Roofing. A sampled guest desktop document navigation/reload to lesson30 now works with DevTools visibly Offline; browser-restart, audio availability and physical-device acceptance remain open. Downloaded availability is specific to the current browser and verified saved files; do not advertise full offline acceptance from source presence or installation alone.

These references informed vocabulary and the need to confirm site/product-specific instructions. They are not reviewer approval of these original passages, and no source procedure is presented as a universal rule.
