# Reader → Tutor caption copy

## Primary 42-second cut

1. Reading another language shouldn’t stop at translation.
2. Start with something real.
3. Tap any word.
4. Understand what it means in this sentence.
5. Ask Tutor why it works that way.
6. Save what matters.
7. Practice it before you forget.
8. Understand it. Ask about it. Remember it. Use it.

## Short alternates

- Translation gives you a word. Context gives you the language.
- Tap the word. Keep the sentence. Ask the better question.
- From “I recognize it” to “I can use it.”

## Accessibility

- Burn captions into social cuts and also export an `.srt`.
- Use sentence case, two lines maximum, and at least 4.5:1 contrast.
- Do not rely on audio, cursor position, or color alone to explain the interaction.
