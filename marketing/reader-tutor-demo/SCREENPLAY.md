Target length: 38 seconds. Capture from an isolated preview with a fresh local profile and
nonpersonal sample content. This is source material only; publishing requires separate approval.

| Time   | Picture                                           | Interaction                                           | Caption / narration                                       |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0–4s   | Language Threshold welcome, clean mobile viewport | —                                                     | Reading another language shouldn’t stop at translation.   |
| 4–9s   | Reader-first welcome                              | Tap **Start Reading**                                 | Start with a real passage. No account or upload required. |
| 9–14s  | Sample passage in Reader                          | Tap **conviven** in the Spanish sentence              | Tap any word.                                             |

| 28–33s | Tutor’s real streamed response                    | Pause on the sentence-specific explanation            | Keep the context. Go deeper.                              |
| 33–37s | Word Card / My Vocab confirmation                 | Save the word, then tap **Study your saved words**    | Save what matters.                                        |
| 37–42s | Flashcards, saved-word deck visible               | Open the saved-word deck                              | Practice it before you forget.                            |
| Final  | Simple product lockup                             | —                                                     | Understand it. Ask about it. Remember it. Use it.         |

Capture requirements:

- Use the real application and a real Tutor response.
- Do not show a personal account, production-user data, console, secrets, or browser errors.
