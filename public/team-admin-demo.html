Open team setup

Admin walkthrough: Seats → Employees → Modules → Certificate rule → Send invitations → Track progress.

Why send the admin an email?

It gives the company a direct entry point, lists the assigned modules and seat count, and explains the 80% certificate option before anyone is invited. A weekly optional summary can report new joins, active minutes, modules practiced, tests due, and certificates issued.

The email is a link and summary. The module content and employee data remain in authenticated app pages.
To: Employee · Subject: Start Roofing Foreman Spanish
Your company assigned you Roofing Foreman Spanish.

Practice roofing materials, tear-off, installation, safety communication, and closeout on your own schedule. Your company chose the certificate goal: finish the required lessons and pass the final test at 80% or higher.

Open your module

Your admin sees completion and practice time by module. Your private AI conversations are not shared.

Employee invitation rule

Each invitation is tied to one named employee and one reserved seat. The link expires after seven days, can be regenerated by the admin, and never exposes another employee's progress.
